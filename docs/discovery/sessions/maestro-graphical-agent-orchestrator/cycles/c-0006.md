## Correction to c-0005

c-0005 recorded that forensics had **disproved** the orphan-process claim, and
concluded that "no persistence mechanism exists in the repo." That conclusion
was wrong, and it was wrong in the direction that matters.

Checkpoints are immutable, so the correction lands here.

What c-0005 got right: the dialog names Maestro because `scripts/build-app.sh`
clones WezTerm into `/Applications/Maestro.app` under
`CFBundleIdentifier com.jdylanmc.maestro`.

What c-0005 got wrong: it inferred from the absence of a launch agent, a
`launchd` plist, or a `nohup` call that nothing survives application close. It
searched for a persistence *mechanism authored in this repository* and, finding
none, declared the behavior impossible. The actual mechanism is an external
dependency the launcher shells out to, and no amount of reading this
repository's own source would have surfaced it. **Absence of evidence was
treated as evidence of absence.**

The correct method was to observe the running system. That is what produced the
answer in this cycle.

## Evidence gathered

Recorded in full in `evidence.md` under the c-0006 headings. In summary: a live
process tree rooted at a detached `herdr server` daemon kept two Copilot
Sessions and their Model Context Protocol servers alive for two days after the
graphical host exited, and macOS attributed their permission prompts to Maestro
through inherited responsibility.
