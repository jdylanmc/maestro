## What was recorded, and why it is not a duplicate of n-0011

Bounded read-only research ran first so the node would not enter as empty fog.
Zellij is **0.45.0, MIT licensed**, a Homebrew formula with one dependency, and
**not installed** on this machine.

The finding that justifies a separate node is that **Zellij trades in the
opposite direction to cmux**:

- **Its plugins are WebAssembly modules that render first-class user-interface
  panes**, and Zellij's own interface is built with that same system. A Fleet
  panel or subagent tree would be a native component rather than a script drawing
  into a terminal pane. This is stronger precisely where c-0021 was blocked: that
  cycle could not test cmux's custom sidebars at all, because they live outside
  the approved isolation path, and cmux's own sidebar language is beta.
- **It gives far less away.** cmux ships Attention, agent hooks for sixteen
  agents including Copilot, notifications, a resource meter, a file tree, and
  workspace metadata. Zellij has **no agent awareness whatsoever**. Eight of the
  nine items in c-0022's "already delivered" column move back into the build
  column.
- **It ships session resurrection**, restoring panes, tabs, and running commands
  across terminal closure and reboot. It is the first candidate host with
  anything in the neighbourhood of Park - the capability c-0022 identified as the
  largest remaining build item, and the one thing cmux philosophically refuses.
- **It is MIT.** cmux ships authentication and billing surfaces and its licensing
  was verified in neither c-0021 nor c-0022. Under the wrap decision this matters
  less than it would have under the rejected fork, but it is unrecorded and it is
  the class of constraint that binds late.

So the trade to be measured is **batteries-included but closed and shallowly
extensible, against a primitive that is open and deeply extensible.**

## A third route class

The c-0022 route-class distinction has two values - terminal-hosted and
application - and **does not cover Zellij**, which is a multiplexer running
inside the operator's own terminal. It owns no window, has no renderer, and
exposes **no accessibility tree of its own** - the property that capped the
WezTerm route at a researched 40-50%. Its seam is the Copilot CLI like the other
terminal-hosted routes, but its presentation and automation surfaces are its
command-line interface and its plugin API.

This is added to the pending `/domain-mapping` packet rather than settled here.
